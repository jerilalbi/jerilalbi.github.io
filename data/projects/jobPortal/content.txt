This is a full-stack job portal built with React.js, Node.js, and MongoDB. It provides role-based access for three types of users: Employers can post jobs, manage applicants, and view their resumes; Job Seekers can search for jobs, apply directly, and track their application status; and Admins can approve or disapprove job postings to maintain platform quality. The system also includes secure authentication with JWT and supports file upload and preview for resumes, ensuring a smooth and practical recruitment workflow.

Features:
- User authentication and authorization using JWT.
- Apply for jobs.
- View and download resumes.

Technologies Used:
Frontend: React.js
Backend: Express.js, Node.js
Database: MongoDB
